# THE REDSHIRE RESCUE — a Snowmoon story

Adaptation of the rescue-heist sequence from Chapter 26 of *Snowmoon* by Vitalik Buterin (GPL v3).
Original narration written for this film (not quoted from the novel).

**Format:** 16:9, ~90 seconds, 9 shots × ~10s
**Voice:** narrator (Somber Monument), narration-only mix
**Captions:** burned-in, DejaVu Sans Bold 44, white → yellow karaoke, bottom-center

---

## Shot 1 — The occupied city (0:00–0:10)
**Narration:** "Redshire. A city under occupation. Tonight, its streetlights burn for someone else's curfew."
**Visual:** Wide aerial night shot. A grand presidential residence on a hill above a sleeping city. Four small drones slip in formation under moonlight.

## Shot 2 — The dark room (0:10–0:20)
**Narration:** "In a dark room far from here, a boy named Zei flies with other people's eyes. A squad of drones, one rope, one balcony — one boy to bring home."
**Visual:** Teenage boy seen from behind at a console, face lit teal by a wall of tactical screens: a drone map, a balcony marker, a yellow triangle icon.

## Shot 3 — The balcony (0:20–0:30)
**Narration:** "The target steps out — Tafindel, the president's son. Then hands grab him from behind. He's dragged back inside. And the night sky starts shooting."
**Visual:** A boy steps onto a grand balcony at night — then shadowy hands seize him and yank him inside. Above, tracer fire rips across the sky.

## Shot 4 — The plan dies (0:30–0:40)
**Narration:** "Three drones down. The fourth — the one carrying the rope — takes a direct hit... and blooms into fire. The plan is dead."
**Visual:** The console room. On the tactical screen, the rope-drone's icon turns into a red X and fades out. Zei grips the desk, shoulders slumping. (Re-staged from the novel's midair explosion.)

## Shot 5 — Watch the lights (0:40–0:50)
**Narration:** "A new voice on the comm: 'Fly low — through the streets, that's where their aim is weakest. And watch the lights.' Across the city, the streetlights begin to strobe, wild and chaotic."
**Visual:** Surviving drones dive between buildings at street level. Every streetlight starts strobing out of sync; enemy drones fire wildly into the confusion.

## Shot 6 — The crash (0:50–1:00)
**Narration:** "'I need a crash at these coordinates.' A decoy drone slams into the residence wall at full speed. Fire. Smoke. Cover."
**Visual:** Street level below the residence: a massive smoke plume billows across the facade, fire glowing behind it, embers drifting down. The aftermath of the decoy crash. (Re-staged from the novel's wall impact.)

## Shot 7 — Hold on (1:00–1:10)
**Narration:** "Two figures in thermal cloaks climb onto the drones' backs. No rope. No backup plan. Just... hold on."
**Visual:** Through the smoke, two cloaked figures (faces hidden) climb onto the backs of two hovering drones. Rotors spin up, glowing.

## Shot 8 — The sea (1:10–1:20)
**Narration:** "Over the rooftops, they jump clear — the empty drones fly on as decoys. A car waits in the dark. A shoreline. A sea drone beyond the waves."
**Visual:** Night seascape. A small sea drone speeds across dark waves, city lights shrinking on the horizon, moonlit wake trailing behind.

## Shot 9 — Dawn (1:20–1:30)
**Narration:** "By dawn, the boy is safe on a foreign coast. And the whole world learns what Redshire learned tonight: they can be beaten."
**Visual:** The console room. The boy leans back in his chair as dawn light cuts through the blinds. The main screen glows green: a coastline map, a single checkmark.
